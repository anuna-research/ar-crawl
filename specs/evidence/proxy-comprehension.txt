Reader response:
The intent is to let operators choose outbound proxy addresses while ensuring requests never silently bypass that choice.

With a configured dead proxy and a reachable origin, the request fails through the proxy; it must not retry directly. Rotation may select another configured proxy, but its exact policy is governed by REQ-102.

REQ-101 is the authority for CLI/config/environment precedence. The Orientation identifies that authority but does not state the precedence order, so a conflicting CLI/config/environment configuration cannot be resolved from this Orientation alone.

CON-102 governs authenticated SOCKS5: the HTTP adaptor supports it through curl; Playwright rejects it before creating a browser. ADR-101 establishes the broader curl/native-browser composition.

Malformed endpoints fail before requests, proxy credentials stay out of diagnostics, and unsupported services require explicit use_proxy:false.
